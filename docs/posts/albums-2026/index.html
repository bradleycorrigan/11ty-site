My favourite albums of 2026 💿

19 November 2026 · about 2 minutes music

Ranking in no particular order since I can't commit to a decision

Popstar Tinashe

This album just feels so fresh and funky I have so much fun listening to it, there's so many songs that stand up by themselves. Sampling Chillin' from Lady Gaga was a great choice.

Too Easy, Nobody Wanna Dance Anymore, and Brainrot

Dancing on the Wall Muna

Muna enter their angsty era and it's very fun. The Hannah Einbinder cameo, the collision of worlds. Genuinely such a great album.

Big Stick, Eastside Girls, and Wannabeher

Music, Fashion, Film Charli xcx

Lowkenuinely worried that the comedown from (overdue?) mainstream success might make this album suck big time, and I'm still mad about the ticket situation, but yes, this album banged regardless.

Camera, 2007, and Yeah

Wuthering Heights Charli xcx

At least the album was good, hey?

Dying for You, Always Everywhere, and Chains of Love

Big Disgrace Haute and Freddy

Perhaps the most joy I've felt listening to a new album in a long time. They felt so new and right straight away. I'm excited to hear more from these two.

Femme Hysteria, Scantily Clad, and Anti-Superstar

Midnight Sun: Girls Trip Zara Larsson

I've never been that into her but she really decided to step it up!!!!!

Eurosummer w Shakira, Midnight Sun, and Crush

She's the Best Troye Sivan

Finally a letter to the divas.

Canada Dry, Troye Meets World, and Party

New Day Liz Cooper

Another entry in the black and white album cover revival. A great, introspective, almost delicate album, from a criminally overlooked artist.

Go Outside, Wave to Frank, IDK, and Loss of Signal

I Think I Spoke Too Soon Rachel Chinouriri

Rachel!!! You are that girl.

One Song Away from Crying, AAAHHHH!!!, and Crash this Car
